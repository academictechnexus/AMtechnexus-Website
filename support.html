Frequently Asked Questions

How do I add a new product to NexusPOS? ▼
Go to Inventory → Add Product → enter name, category, price and low-stock threshold → Save. Appears on billing screen immediately.
What happens if the internet goes down during billing? ▼
NexusPOS works fully offline. Bills sync to cloud the moment internet is restored. No data is ever lost.
How do I transfer stock between branches? ▼
Stock Transfer → select item → choose quantity → choose direction (B1→B2 or B2→B1) → Confirm. Updates instantly on both branches.
Can I reprint an old invoice? ▼
Yes. Sales History → find invoice by date or number → click Print. Every invoice is stored permanently.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is the review collection FTC-compliant? ▼
Yes. Every rating — 1 star to 5 stars — goes through the same review flow and every rating also adds to a private feedback channel. We never block customers from leaving public reviews based on their star rating. Fully FTC-compliant.
How does the DM Agent handle uncertain messages? ▼
The AI escalates anything it's not confident about. It never guesses or fabricates an answer. When it escalates, you receive a real SMS and optionally a phone call — so you never miss an urgent customer situation.
Can I publish different content to different social platforms? ▼
Yes. Use the "All Platforms" composer to post to all 9 simultaneously, or switch to a per-platform tab to craft platform-specific content with different text, images and formatting.
What is the difference between Starter and Pro? ▼
Starter covers 3 social platforms, 30 AI images/month and monthly AI insights — perfect for a single-location business starting out. Pro unlocks all 9 platforms, 150 AI images/month, autonomous DM Agent, real SMS/call alerts, competitor tracking and the AI Advisor.
