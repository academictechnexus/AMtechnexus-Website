Frequently Asked Questions

How do I add a new product? ▼
Go to Inventory → Add Product → enter name, category, price and low-stock threshold → Save.
Can I change the GST rate? ▼
Yes. Admin Panel → GST Settings → change the rate. Existing invoices keep their original rate.
What if internet goes down? ▼
NexusPOS works fully offline. Bills sync to cloud the moment internet is restored. No data is ever lost.
How do I reprint an old invoice? ▼
Sales History → find invoice by date or number → click Print. Every invoice is stored permanently.
How to transfer stock between branches? ▼
Stock Transfer → select item → choose quantity → choose direction → Confirm. Updates instantly on both branches.

Frequently Asked Questions

How do I create a job card? ▼
Job Cards → New Job Card → enter plate number → auto-fills history → add complaint → assign technician → Save.
How do parts deduct from inventory? ▼
When closing a job card, select parts used. System deducts stock and adds cost to the final bill automatically.
How does the GST invoice work for a garage? ▼
Generates GST-compliant invoice showing labour and parts charges separately with applicable GST rates.
Can customers track their vehicle status? ▼
Yes. On Pro and Business plans, customers receive WhatsApp updates at each stage automatically.
How do I add spare parts to inventory? ▼
Inventory → Add Part → enter name, part number, purchase price, selling price and stock. Set a low-stock alert.
