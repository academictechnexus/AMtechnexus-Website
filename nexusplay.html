FAQ

Common questions

How does split payment work? ▼
When you book, you can split the total evenly among your group. Each player gets their own payment link on WhatsApp or SMS, so nobody has to front the money and chase everyone else afterward.
What's the Open Games Hub? ▼
It's a board of games other players have organised and still have open spots in. Short a player for your own game? Post it there. Looking for a game to join? Browse what's already happening near you.
Can I manage more than one venue? ▼
Yes. The venue owner dashboard supports multiple turfs or arenas under one login, so you can switch between locations, courts and bookings without separate accounts for each one.
How do venue owners get paid? ▼
Your choice per venue: accept direct UPI payments with zero platform fee, or offer online card/UPI checkout through NexusPlay for a smoother customer experience.
Is booking free for players? ▼
Yes, browsing and booking is free for players. You pay the venue for your slot, either online through the app or directly at the venue, depending on what that venue offers.
What sports and venues does NexusPlay support? ▼
Football turfs, cricket nets and box cricket, badminton and pickleball courts, and multi-sport arenas, anywhere courts and slots need to be booked and managed.
