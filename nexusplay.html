Common questions

Straight answers for players and venue owners.

Do you take commission on online bookings? ▼
No. NexusPlay does not take commission on online bookings. Venue owners keep the booking amount.
Can players book slots online? ▼
Yes. Players can browse venues, check available slots, and book online.
Who gets the WhatsApp booking confirmation? ▼
The venue owner. When a player books a slot online, NexusPlay sends a WhatsApp confirmation to the owner with the booking details.
What is Host a game? ▼
If a slot is still unbooked, you can open a hosted game on that slot. Other players can join, which helps fill empty times instead of leaving them vacant.
Can owners manage walk-ins too? ▼
Yes. Walk-in bookings can be added and managed in the same platform as online bookings, so NexusPlay works as your full booking management tool.
What kind of performance analysis do owners get? ▼
Owners get in-depth views of revenue, occupancy, idle slots, and breakdowns by sport or court, so they can track how the venue is performing over time.
Are reviews and ratings available? ▼
Yes. Players can leave ratings and reviews after they play, and others can use them when choosing a venue.
What venues does this work for? ▼
Football turfs, cricket nets, badminton and pickleball courts, and other sports arenas that take slot bookings.
