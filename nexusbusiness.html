FAQ

Common questions

Is review collection FTC-compliant? ▼
Yes. Every rating — 1 star to 5 stars — goes through the same review flow and every rating also adds to a private feedback channel. We never block customers from leaving public reviews based on their star rating. Fully FTC-compliant.
How does the AI DM Agent handle uncertain messages? ▼
The AI escalates anything it's not confident about — it never guesses or fabricates an answer. When it escalates, you receive a real SMS and optionally a phone call, so you never miss an urgent customer situation.
Can I publish different content to different social platforms? ▼
Yes. Use the "All Platforms" composer to post to all 9 simultaneously, or switch to a per-platform tab to craft specific content with different text, images and formatting for each platform.
How does AI image generation work? ▼
NexusBusiness Suite uses an auto-fallback chain: Cloudflare Workers AI first (free), then Gemini, then OpenAI — always finding the best available model. Chat naturally to describe what you want, upload real photos to mix in, and generate multiple variations without losing your conversation.
What's the difference between Starter and Pro? ▼
Starter covers 3 social platforms, 30 AI images/month and core review features — perfect for a single-location business. Pro unlocks all 9 platforms, 150 AI images/month, the autonomous DM Agent, real SMS/call alerts, competitor tracking and the AI Advisor.
Do I need to be technical to set this up? ▼
Not at all. Setup takes under 30 minutes with our guided onboarding. Connect Google Business with one click, connect social platforms with OAuth, and print your QR code. Our team is on WhatsApp to assist with any step.
