FAQ

Common questions

Is the review collection FTC-compliant? ▼
Yes. Every rating — 1 star to 5 stars — goes through the same review flow and every rating adds to a private feedback channel. We never block or filter customers from leaving public reviews based on their star rating. This makes NexusBusiness Suite fully compliant with FTC guidelines on review solicitation.
How does the AI DM Agent handle uncertain situations? ▼
The AI is trained to escalate anything it's not confident about. It never guesses or fabricates an answer. When it escalates, you receive a real SMS message and optionally a phone call — not just a push notification — so you never miss an urgent customer situation.
Can I publish different content to different social platforms? ▼
Yes. You can use the "All Platforms" composer to post identical content everywhere at once, or switch to a per-platform tab to craft platform-specific posts. Instagram, Facebook, LinkedIn, X, Threads, Pinterest, Bluesky, Telegram and Google Business are all supported, each with their own formatting.
How does the AI image generation work? ▼
NexusBusiness Suite uses an auto-fallback chain: Cloudflare Workers AI first (free), then Gemini, then OpenAI — always finding the best available model. You can chat naturally to describe what you want, upload your own photos to mix in, and generate multiple variations without losing your conversation context.
What's the difference between Starter and Pro? ▼
Starter covers 3 social platforms, 30 AI images/month and monthly AI insights — perfect for a single-location business. Pro unlocks all 9 platforms, 150 AI images/month, the autonomous DM Agent, real SMS/call alerts, competitor tracking, and the AI Advisor for chatting with your review data.
Do I need to be technical to set this up? ▼
Not at all. Setup takes under 30 minutes with our guided onboarding. Connect your Google Business profile with one click, connect social platforms with OAuth, and print your QR code. Our team is available on WhatsApp to assist with any step.
