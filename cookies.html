This Cookie Policy explains how AM Technexus Labs (“AM Technexus,” “we,” “us,” or “our”) uses cookies and similar technologies on amtechnexus.com and related pages. It should be read with our Privacy Policy.

1. What are cookies?

Cookies are small text files stored on your device when you visit a website. Similar technologies include local storage, session storage, and pixels. They help sites remember preferences, keep sessions secure, and understand how pages are used.

2. How we use cookies

We use cookies and similar technologies to:

  • Make the website and product interfaces work correctly
  • Remember basic preferences such as navigation or UI state where needed
  • Protect against abuse and diagnose technical issues
  • Understand aggregate traffic and improve content (if analytics tools are enabled)

3. Types of cookies we may use

  • Essential cookies — required for security, load balancing, authentication, and core site functions. The site may not work properly without them.
  • Functional cookies — remember choices that improve your experience (for example, UI preferences).
  • Analytics cookies — help us understand which pages are visited and how visitors move through the site, in aggregate form. We enable these only when an analytics tool is active on the site.
  • Marketing cookies — used only if we run advertising or remarketing campaigns. We do not currently rely on advertising cookies on the marketing site by default.

4. Third-party cookies

Some pages load third-party resources (for example fonts, video players, or messaging widgets). Those providers may set their own cookies under their policies. We do not control third-party cookies.

5. Managing cookies

You can control cookies through your browser settings. You can usually block or delete cookies, or set your browser to notify you before a cookie is stored. If you block essential cookies, parts of the website or product login experience may not work.

Browser help pages typically explain cookie controls for Chrome, Safari, Firefox, and Edge.

6. Local storage

Our site and products may use browser local storage or session storage for similar purposes as cookies, such as keeping a session active or storing temporary UI state. You can clear this data in your browser settings.

7. Updates

We may update this Cookie Policy when our practices or tools change. The “Last updated” date at the top will be revised when we do.
