This Privacy Policy explains how AM Technexus Labs (“AM Technexus,” “we,” “us,” or “our”) collects, uses, stores, and shares information when you visit amtechnexus.com, contact us, or use our products and services.

1. Who we are

AM Technexus Labs is a software company based in Tamil Nadu, India. We build business products including NexusPOS, NexusAI, NexusBilling, NexusApartments, NexusRevu, NexusBusiness Suite, NexusSEO+, and NexusPlay.

Questions about this policy: support@amtechnexus.com or +91 88078 10394.

2. Information we collect

Depending on how you interact with us, we may collect:

  • Contact details — name, email, phone number, business name, and message content when you request a demo, email us, or message us on WhatsApp.
  • Account and product data — login details, business settings, billing records, inventory, bookings, customer records, review drafts, and other data you enter while using our products.
  • Usage and device data — pages visited, approximate location derived from IP address, browser type, device type, and diagnostic logs needed to keep the site and products working.
  • Payment-related information — invoice details and payment status. Card or UPI payments (if used) are processed by third-party payment providers; we do not store full card numbers on our servers.

3. How we use information

  • Respond to enquiries, demos, and support requests
  • Provide, maintain, secure, and improve our websites and products
  • Send service messages such as booking alerts, job updates, or account notices you enable
  • Process invoices and commercial agreements
  • Detect abuse, prevent fraud, and meet legal obligations
  • Send product or marketing updates only where permitted and with an easy way to opt out

4. How we share information

We do not sell your personal information. We may share information with:

  • Service providers who help us host, email, message (including WhatsApp Business tools), process payments, or analyse site performance, under confidentiality obligations
  • Your organisation’s authorised users when data belongs to a business account you control
  • Authorities when required by law or to protect rights, safety, and security

If you use a product that sends WhatsApp messages (for example booking confirmations to venue owners, or customer updates), those messages are delivered through WhatsApp’s platform under WhatsApp’s own terms and policies.

5. Retention

We keep personal information only as long as needed for the purposes above, including support, accounting, dispute resolution, and legal requirements. You may ask us to delete account data subject to lawful retention needs.

6. Security

We use reasonable technical and organisational measures to protect information. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security.

7. Your choices and rights

Subject to applicable Indian law, you may request access, correction, or deletion of personal information we hold about you, or ask questions about how it is used. Contact support@amtechnexus.com. We may need to verify your identity before acting on a request.

8. Children

Our website and products are intended for businesses and adult users. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 18.

9. Cookies

We use cookies and similar technologies as described in our Cookie Policy.

10. Changes

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The “Last updated” date at the top will change when we do. Continued use of our website or services after an update means you accept the revised policy.
