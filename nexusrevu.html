NEXUSREVU

From QR to Google review in under a minute

Customers scan your QR, answer 3 of your questions, and get a review that feels human, different every time, ready to paste on Google. If the feedback is negative, WhatsApp to your business comes first so you can fix it privately.

How it works

Built for the counter. No app download. No complicated flow.

Step 1
Scan the QR

Customer scans the QR at your counter or table. Opens instantly on their phone.

Step 2
Answer 3 questions

You set up to 10 questions. Each visit, they get a different set of 3, so no two reviews sound the same.

Step 3
A human-sounding review

From their answers, NexusRevu drafts a natural review, not a copy-paste template. They tap Post, Google opens, they paste and publish.

Step 4
Negative? WhatsApp first

If the draft is negative, WhatsApp to your business is the main option so you can resolve it. They can still post online if they want.

Common questions

Straight answers about getting more Google reviews with NexusRevu.

How to get more Google reviews with NexusRevu? ▼
Place your NexusRevu QR at the counter or table. Happy customers scan, answer 3 questions, get a natural review draft, and paste it on Google in under a minute.
Do customers need to download an app? ▼
No. They scan the QR and continue on their phone browser. No app download is required.
What happens if the feedback is negative? ▼
WhatsApp to your business comes first so you can resolve it privately. The customer can still choose to post online if they want.
Will every review sound the same? ▼
No. You set up to 10 questions. Each visit, customers get a different set of 3, so reviews stay human and varied.
