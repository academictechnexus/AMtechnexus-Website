FAQ

Common questions

What's the difference between SEO, AEO and GEO? ▼
SEO improves visibility in traditional search engines and organic results. AEO structures your content for direct answers, questions and conversational discovery, think voice search and chat assistants. GEO improves the content and digital signals that AI-powered discovery systems, like ChatGPT and Gemini, use to understand and recommend your business. NexusSEO+ runs all three together because they reinforce each other.
Do you guarantee rankings? ▼
No. We don't make unrealistic ranking guarantees, and you should be cautious of anyone who does. We commit to executing the agreed work professionally, measuring progress transparently, and continuously improving your digital foundation.
What is Nexus Revu and why is it complimentary? ▼
Nexus Revu is our Google review collection and management layer, part of the NexusBusiness Suite. Since reviews directly influence local SEO and AI trust signals, Growth and Enterprise NexusSEO+ clients get complimentary access as part of the programme.
How long until I see results? ▼
Month 1 is audit, baseline measurement and roadmap. Month 2 is technical, on-page, local SEO and AEO/GEO foundations. Month 3 onward is where content, authority and AI visibility compound. Most clients see meaningful movement by month 3, with results continuing to build after that.
Do I need to provide content, or do you create it? ✚
We prepare a prioritised content and page-optimisation plan and support implementation, we're not limited to handing you a report. For social media, we align the editorial calendar with your available photo and video assets and agreed priorities.
Can I switch plans as my business grows? ▼
Yes. Most clients start on Launch or Growth and move up as their content and AI-visibility needs expand. Multi-location and franchise businesses typically move to Enterprise for coordinated, market-by-market reporting.
