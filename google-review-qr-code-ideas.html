Google review QR code ideas

A review QR only helps if people see it — and if what happens after the scan is worth finishing. Placement matters. What the QR opens matters more. NexusRevu turns the scan into 3 questions and a human-sounding draft, not a blank Google box.

Why a Google review QR works

People abandon review requests when they have to open Maps, search your business name, and write from scratch. A QR removes the search step. A NexusRevu QR also removes the blank-page problem: three short answers become a natural draft ready to paste on Google.

For the full review growth system, read how to get more Google reviews. For the ask itself, see how to ask customers for Google reviews.

Best places to put a review QR

Counter / billing desk

Highest conversion for most shops

Customers are already standing still and talking to staff. Perfect moment for a polite ask and a scan.

Table tent / menu insert

Great for restaurants and cafes

Place it near the bill or dessert menu, when the visit is ending on a good note.

Printed receipt or invoice

Useful for workshops and clinics

Add a line like “Scan to leave a Google review” under the total. Keep the QR large enough to scan easily.

Waiting area or exit

Good when visits take longer

Salons, clinics, and service centres can place a clean stand near the exit path.

WhatsApp follow-up

For people who leave in a hurry

Send the same review link once, soon after the visit. Do not spam.

QR design tips that get scans

  • Make the QR large enough — tiny codes get ignored
  • Add a clear headline: “Leave us a Google review”
  • Mention time: “Takes under a minute”
  • Keep branding clean; do not surround it with five competing offers
  • Test the scan from Android and iPhone before printing

Common QR mistakes

  • Linking to your homepage instead of the review flow
  • Printing a QR that opens a broken or outdated page
  • Hiding the QR behind the cash register
  • Expecting the QR to work with zero staff ask
  • Opening a blank Google form — customers still freeze at “write a review”

Why NexusRevu stands out

Printing a QR to Google is table stakes. NexusRevu is what happens after the scan: customers answer 3 of your questions (from up to 10 you set), get a human-sounding draft that is different each visit, and paste it on Google. If feedback is negative, WhatsApp to your business can come first — covered in handling negative reviews.

Frequently asked questions

Should the QR go straight to Google? ▼
A direct Google link works, but many customers still freeze at the writing step. A NexusRevu QR opens a short guided flow — three answers become a draft — so more scans become published reviews.
How many QR placements do I need? ▼
Start with one high-traffic spot like the counter. Add table tents or receipts only after the first placement is working.
Can I use the same QR on print and WhatsApp? ▼
Yes. One review link everywhere keeps tracking and training simple for your team.
